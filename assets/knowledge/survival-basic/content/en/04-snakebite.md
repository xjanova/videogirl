# Snakebite: what to do right away

Move the person away from the snake, keep them still and calm, moving as little as possible · Take off rings, bracelets and watches before the area swells · Keep the bitten limb still with a splint, then get to hospital fast or call 1669 · Antivenom at the hospital is the main treatment
Source: WHO Snakebite envenoming (fact sheet) · Avau et al. 2016, The Treatment of Snake Bites in a First Aid Setting: A Systematic Review (PLoS Neglected Tropical Diseases)

# Snakebite: what not to do

Do not cut the wound, suck out venom, tie a tight tourniquet, put ice or herbal remedies on it, and do not waste time chasing or catching the snake · These do not help and raise the risk of infection, bleeding or tissue damage
Source: WHO Snakebite envenoming (fact sheet) · Avau et al. 2016 (PLoS NTD) · Instituto Clodomiro Picado, University of Costa Rica (snakebite first aid advice)
