# Emergency numbers in Thailand: calling for help

1669 medical emergencies (National Institute for Emergency Medicine), free nationwide · 191 police emergencies · 199 fire · 1784 disaster hotline (Department of Disaster Prevention and Mitigation)
When you call, say: what happened, where (coordinates or a landmark), how many people are hurt, how they are, and a number to call you back on
Source: National Institute for Emergency Medicine (NIEMS) · Department of Disaster Prevention and Mitigation
