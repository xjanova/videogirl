# Lost in the forest: stop, do not keep walking

As soon as you know you are lost, stop, calm down, look around, then plan (STOP: Stop, Think, Observe, Plan) · Walking on without knowing the way usually takes you further from where searchers will look · If someone knows where you went in, staying put helps them find you sooner
Make yourself easy to find: stay in an open spot visible from far away, use bright clothing or cloth, and blow a whistle in repeated bursts (it carries further than shouting and tires you less)
Source: the STOP rule and "stay put when lost", taught by search and rescue groups, e.g. Hug-a-Tree and Survive (USA)

# Phone battery low, weak signal: how do I call for help?

Turn the screen brightness down, close apps you are not using, and switch on battery saver · If the signal is too weak for a call, send a text message: short texts often get through when calls do not · Give your location (coordinates from the map app, or a landmark), how you are, and how many people are with you
Source: Ready.gov (US FEMA) advice on communicating in an emergency: text messages often get through when calls are jammed
