# Water from a stream or spring: can I drink it, how long do I boil it?

Water from streams, ponds or other natural sources can carry germs even when it looks clear · The surest way is a rolling boil for 1 minute (3 minutes above about 2,000 metres), then let it cool before drinking · If the water is cloudy, let it settle and strain it through a clean cloth before boiling · Boiling kills germs but does not remove chemicals in the water
Source: CDC (US Centers for Disease Control and Prevention), Making Water Safe in an Emergency and Boil Water Advisory
