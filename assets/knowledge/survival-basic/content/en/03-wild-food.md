# Wild mushrooms and plants: how can I tell if they are safe to eat?

The rule of Thailand's Department of Disease Control: if you do not know it, if you are not sure, do not pick it and do not eat it · Some poisonous mushrooms look very much like edible ones, especially when young, and cannot be told apart by eye · Folk tests such as a silver spoon are not reliable · Boiling, frying or grilling does not destroy the toxins
Source: Department of Disease Control, Ministry of Public Health, Thailand · Department of Medical Sciences, Thailand (folk tests for poisonous mushrooms are unreliable)

# Ate wild mushrooms and feel sick

Nausea, vomiting, stomach pain or diarrhoea after eating wild mushrooms: go to hospital or call 1669 at once, and take any leftover mushrooms for the doctor to see · Symptoms can start several hours after eating, and some toxins cause liver or kidney failure, so do not wait it out at home
Source: Department of Disease Control, Ministry of Public Health, Thailand (poisonous mushroom warnings in the rainy season)
