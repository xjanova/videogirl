# Flash floods: camping beside a stream, wading through water

In heavy rain, do not camp or sleep beside streams, gullies or dry stream beds: water from upstream can arrive even when it is not raining where you are · Warning signs: the water turns muddy, rises fast, carries branches, or you hear a roar upstream. Go to high ground at once and leave your things · Do not wade through moving water: about 15 cm (6 inches) can knock a person down
Source: U.S. National Weather Service, Turn Around Don't Drown · flash flood warnings of Thailand's Department of Disaster Prevention and Mitigation
